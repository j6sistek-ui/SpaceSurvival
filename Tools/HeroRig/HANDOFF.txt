REPLACEMENT SQUIRREL RIG - 2026-09-23

Open SquirrelHero_Rigged.blend in Blender. Press Space in the viewport to preview
the idle tail. The Action Editor contains Tail_Idle, Tail_Walk, Tail_Run,
Tail_JumpStart, Tail_JumpAir, Tail_JumpLand and Rig_Check_Not_Gameplay.
Preview GIFs isolate the tail on a stationary body; they are not body locomotion.

The selected source is the owner's Tripo sci-fi_squirrel_3d_model from Unreal:
75,183 triangles, one material, original UVs and geometry preserved. The rig has
69 bones: the existing 60-bone mannequin-style body plus root, seven tail bones,
and backpack. Tail weights no longer include thighs. The rigid backpack core is
blended into its attached straps/cloth. The source import remains unchanged.

FBX is supplied for Unreal; GLB and the packed Blender file include textures.
The two TGA files are supplied alongside FBX. Prefer the existing Unreal material
when importing a review copy so material settings match the original import.

The six Tail_ FBX files contain reference-pose body tracks and tail motion.
They must be composed as local-space additive animation against the reference
pose, or layered on tail_01 and descendants after body retargeting. Do not play
them as full-body walking/running clips. JumpStart -> JumpAir loop -> JumpLand
have matching endpoint poses. Blend between gait/idle layers rather than cutting.

Mannequin-style bone names make retarget setup easier, but this is not the exact
Epic mannequin skeleton or proportions. It requires an IK retarget setup.
No gameplay assets, current hero selection, package, or itch build were replaced.
Native Unreal import/retargeting, actual locomotion integration, collision with
the cockpit/ship, landing timing, and in-game owner acceptance remain open.

Focused checks: stride/crouch/twist poses, normalized maximum-four weights,
source geometry/UV hash, no thigh influence on the tail, rigid pack core shape,
FBX/GLB reimport counts and bone presence, tail loop/transition endpoint checks.
These do not prove production locomotion, automatic obstacle-aware tail physics,
fur simulation, finger-grip quality, or final jump animation quality.
