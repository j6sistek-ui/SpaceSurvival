# Photographic rock material candidate

M_RockPhotographic is an isolated material using Poly Haven Rock Face's original 2K diffuse, ARM and DirectX normal maps. The three source files retain their official byte counts/MD5 and recorded SHA256. Attribution and CC0 provenance are in ../ThirdParty/PolyHaven/RockFace/PROVENANCE.md. No existing asteroid mesh or material assignment was changed.

AuthorRockPhotographic.py creates four candidate assets. Its signed object-local triplanar projection requires no UV changes, remains attached during translation/rotation and perturbs the original local surface normal. Flat normal maps preserve that surface normal. RepeatLocalCm=240 is before actor scale: larger rocks have larger visible features. Shader parameters retain mild color desaturation, cool tint, normal strength0.75, roughness floor0.68 and metallic0. The source metallic channel is zero.

RockPhotographicImport.json and RockPhotographicPersisted.json record actual import and fresh UE5.8.2 readback; all77 existing packages were preserved. Existing asteroid variants remain440triangles with unchanged bounds, zero authored collision and original M_Rock assignments. The material has9 explicit texture samples. Editor shader statistics report448pixel instructions,6samplers and12pixel texture samples including pass overhead; these estimates are not runtime performance measurements.

Final ValidateRockPhotographic.py --preview exited0. Its four native images show the current surface, candidate, camera/light/rock co-rotation, and a large translation of the co-rotated scene. Directional shadows and temporal AA are disabled only in this unsaved studio preview to isolate surface mapping. Review.json records measured pixel differences and hashes; the images are not claimed byte-identical. The candidate shows clear grain/cracks over the existing coarse silhouette. It remains pale under the studio lights and requires actual space lighting/readability review.
