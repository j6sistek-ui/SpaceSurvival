## Option B — wave mode with a speed-gated score and an oxygen scrubber

Logged 2026-09-20. Owner's framing: *an alternate method, to debate another day.*

- **Wave mode only scores points while the ship is holding a certain speed.** Below that threshold,
  points stop accruing.
- **The player can stop fully.** Stopping is permitted, not blocked.
- **The ship's oxygen scrubber only works while passing through space.** A deliberate handwave — the
  owner named it a fake concept, and it is recorded as one rather than dressed up as physics.
- **Sitting still too long therefore starts doing damage.** Stillness has a cost that builds, instead
  of a wall that refuses the input.
- **Obstacles and enemies remain a threat while stopped, but are not always present.** The danger of
  standing still is real but not guaranteed, so it reads as risk rather than as punishment.

### What it is for

It plugs the gap left by loopholes and transitional delays — the states where a player could
otherwise park indefinitely and wait out pressure. It does that **without forcing continuous
motion outright**.

### The open question

Whether it produces the gameplay feel the owner wants. That is a feel test, not an analysis, and it
has not been run.
