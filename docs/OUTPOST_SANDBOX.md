# Wayfarer Exchange: asteroid outpost sandbox

This is a separately authored station design scene for walking, visual review and later editing. It preserves the asteroid setting and adds a five-group market promenade, a circular concourse, engineering, crew quarters, operations, detailed visitor berths, a large planetary archive with seven welcome displays, and a walkable glass observation gallery. It does not replace the current gameplay station, change the normal startup map, update a packaged executable or publish to itch.

The scene is `/Game/OutpostSandbox/L_AsteroidOutpost`. Its current local checkout is `C:/Users/j6sis/SpaceSurvival/Artifacts/Worktrees/asteroid-outpost`. The saved map and licensed assets are local; a Git clone alone cannot reproduce them.

## Open it

- **Walk through it:** double-click [Play Outpost Sandbox.cmd](../Play%20Outpost%20Sandbox.cmd) in the outpost checkout. This starts the map directly at 1600 x 900.
- **Edit it:** double-click [Edit Outpost Sandbox.cmd](../Edit%20Outpost%20Sandbox.cmd) in the same checkout. The map opens in Unreal Editor; use Play to walk and Stop before making saved-level edits.

Both launchers use the existing UE5.8 installation and this checkout's compiled Editor module. They do not build or download assets. The Play launcher runs an Editor-backed game session, not the older `Artifacts/Windows` packaged executable. The Edit launcher selects this map explicitly for that visit; it does not change `EditorStartupMap` or `GameDefaultMap`, which remain `/Game/SpaceSurvival/Maps/Survival`. If the launcher reports a missing module, build this checkout with `Scripts/Build.ps1 -Target Editor`; if it reports a missing map, restore the private authored content rather than opening an unrelated older station.

The sandbox uses its own persistent profile at `Artifacts/Outpost/PlayerProfile`. When a departure terminal opens the gameplay map, that same isolated profile remains active. Continue Survival therefore refers to a checkpoint in this review profile, not a checkpoint from the installed or published game.

Move within roughly three metres of a console and face its access point. A bottom-screen prompt names the focused service. Interaction checks range and line of sight; use the visible prompt rather than pressing through a wall. The cockpit terminal has a shorter 2.5-metre reach.

The entrance and three interior branch doors use proximity sensors. Approach the doorway to open it; they hold while the passage is occupied and slide closed after it clears. An opening button is not required. Actual passage and collision acceptance are tracked with the rest of the sandbox validation.

## Suggested first walk

1. Start on the arrival approach, turn back toward the player ship and find **SHIP FINISH** beside its berth. Visit the two smaller transit berths, then follow the central promenade past the arrival produce and weighing counters, north commercial block, south botany stalls and south machinery group toward the main entrance.
2. Enter the circular concourse. Explore the seven displays around the globe, then visit **FLIGHT ENGINEERING**, then **CREW QUARTERS** for the wardrobe projector and arcade. Finish in **OPERATIONS**. The staircase beside Engineering leads to the glass observation gallery overlooking the berths.
3. Return to the ship's rear ramp and explore the cabin/cockpit route. Use the Free Flight terminal only when ready to leave the design scene; either Survival departures board also leaves this map for the existing game choices.

## Editing and preservation

Edit `/Game/OutpostSandbox/L_AsteroidOutpost`, with Play stopped, and save the level normally. Actor folders separate the ground, exterior, rooms, dressing, services, doors, crew and drones. Keep movement routes, access points and doorway clearances in mind when moving nearby props.

The main authoring recipe rebuilds this generated map from a new blank world. It makes a timestamped map backup under `Artifacts/Outpost` first, but rerunning it still replaces the current composition. Preserve manual changes before requesting regeneration. Original vendor assets are referenced, while outpost geometry and material derivatives live under `/Game/OutpostSandbox`. The scene uses one shared environment; imported example-map environments are not combined with it. The recipe saves an initial map before the detail pass, then saves the integrated result and reloads it for the broader placement audit. A failed detail or audit step does not automatically restore the backup, so a saved map alone is not proof that the full recipe passed.

The earlier `BuildingSandbox.blend` belongs to the five-example building sandbox, not this outpost. Do not push that scene into the outpost map. A Blender round trip needs a fresh export associated with this exact map; this guide does not claim that the outpost's animated actors, Blueprint assemblies or door behavior round-trip through Blender.
